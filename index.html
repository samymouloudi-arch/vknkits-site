03 — F.A.Q

Questions?

Email vknkits@gmail.com or DM @vknkits.

How much does each kit cost?

Each kit is priced at $50.

Are all sales final?

Yes. All sales are final and non-refundable.

Can I exchange my purchase if something is wrong with it?

Absolutely. If your kit arrives damaged or defective, you may exchange it for a new replacement kit. Please contact us within the specified timeframe with proof of the damage, and we’ll be happy to assist.

Do you offer refunds instead of exchanges?

No. While all sales are final, we do offer exchanges for damaged or defective kits only. Refunds are not provided.

Can I choose my team?

That’s the surprise. But you can tell us the teams you do NOT want when you order, and we’ll keep them out of your box.

How does Kit Shuffle work?

Fill out the Kit Shuffle form and we’ll reach out to schedule your swap. All swaps are reviewed, and a $20 fee applies for your new jersey once your swap is approved.
